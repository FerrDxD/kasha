# 🤝 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

**Our Pledge**

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

</div>

---

## 📜 Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment

✅ **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- Avoid jargon when possible, or explain it
- Use gender-neutral language (e.g., "they" instead of "he/she")
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn

✅ **Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences**
- Accept that others may have different approaches
- Learn from diverse perspectives
- Assume good intentions

✅ **Gracefully accepting constructive criticism**
- Listen to feedback without defensiveness
- Ask clarifying questions
- Iterate and improve

✅ **Focusing on what is best for the community**
- Prioritize the project's health over personal preferences
- Make decisions that benefit the most users
- Consider long-term maintainability

✅ **Showing empathy towards other community members**
- Remember that everyone has different circumstances
- Be patient with those learning new skills
- Support others during challenging times

### Examples of unacceptable behavior

❌ **The use of sexualized language or imagery**
- Sexual innuendos or jokes
- Inappropriate comments about appearance
- Unwanted sexual advances

❌ **Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks**
- Name-calling or mocking
- Dismissive language like "RTFM"
- Political or religious debates unrelated to the project

❌ **Public or private harassment**
- Repeated unwanted contact
- Stalking or doxxing
- Threats of violence

❌ **Publishing others' private information**
- Sharing email addresses without consent
- Revealing personal details
- Screenshotting private conversations

❌ **Other unethical or unprofessional conduct**
- Plagiarism or claiming others' work as your own
- Sabotaging others' contributions
- Misrepresenting your experience or credentials

## 🎯 Our Responsibilities

### Project Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- **Clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior**
- Documenting expectations clearly
- Leading by example

- **Enforcing this code of conduct fairly**
- Applying rules consistently
- Considering context before taking action

- **Responding appropriately to unacceptable behavior**
- Investigating reports promptly
- Taking appropriate action
- Following up to prevent recurrence

### Contributors

Contributors are responsible for:

- **Reading and understanding this code of conduct**
- Asking questions if anything is unclear
- Treating others with respect

- **Reporting unacceptable behavior**
- Speaking up when they witness violations
- Supporting those who report issues

- **Accepting feedback gracefully**
- Learning from mistakes
- Improving their behavior

## 📢 Reporting Guidelines

### How to Report

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it:

**Primary Contact:**
- Email: maulanaferdi0678@gmail.com
- Subject: `[KASHA CoC] - [Brief Description]`

**Alternative Contacts:**
- GitHub: Send a private message to any maintainer
- Discord: Direct message a moderator (if applicable)

### What to Include

- Your contact information (if you want a response)
- Description of the incident
- Time and location (when/where it happened)
- Screenshots or logs (if applicable)
- Names of those involved (if comfortable sharing)

### Confidentiality

All reports will be kept confidential to the extent possible. We will:

- Protect your identity unless you consent to disclosure
- Share details only with those who need to know
- Update you on the investigation progress

### Response Timeline

| Time Frame | Action |
|------------|--------|
| Within 24 hours | Acknowledge receipt of report |
| Within 48 hours | Initial investigation and response |
| Within 7 days | Resolution or status update |
| Ongoing | Follow-up to ensure issue is resolved |

## ⚖️ Enforcement

### Consequences

If a violation of this code of conduct is reported, the project team will:

1. **Investigate** the report thoroughly
2. **Determine** appropriate action based on severity
3. **Communicate** decisions to involved parties
4. **Document** the outcome (anonymized if needed)

### Possible Actions

Depending on the severity and context, actions may include:

- **Warning**: Private conversation about the behavior
- **Temporary ban**: Time-limited exclusion from the community
- **Permanent ban**: Permanent removal from the community
- **Content removal**: Deleting inappropriate comments or PRs
- **Public statement**: Acknowledging the issue (if appropriate)

### Appeal Process

If you believe action was taken unfairly:

1. Submit an appeal to the project maintainers
2. Provide context and evidence supporting your case
3. Maintain respectful communication throughout
4. Accept the final decision

## 🌍 Scope

This code of conduct applies to:

- All official KASHA repositories
- Public and private channels (GitHub, Discord, Slack, etc.)
- Meetups, conferences, and events
- Private communication related to the project
- Sponsors and partners interacting with the community

This code of conduct does not apply to:

- Private conversations unrelated to the project
- Other communities or projects
- Personal social media accounts (unless official KASHA accounts)
